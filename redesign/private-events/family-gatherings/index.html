Private Events — Family Gatherings

The people who shaped your life deserve a table worthy of the occasion.

Families come here to celebrate birthdays, anniversaries, retirements, graduations, celebrations of life, and the moments that mark the passage of time together. The occasions themselves may be different, but they all share the same desire: to gather the people who matter most in a place that feels worthy of the moment.

Because the best family gatherings are never really about the event itself. They’re about creating the rare feeling that, for a few hours, everyone made it back to the same table again.
